CENTRALIZED ADVERTISING DATA & REPORTING SYSTEM

AI Meta Ads Dashboard

BUSINESS PROBLEM Monthly reporting requires historical Meta Ads data going back to 2023. Pulling metrics and spend manually, campaign by campaign, month by month, was slow and easy to get wrong.

SYSTEM I BUILT A centralized dashboard that consolidates campaign, spend, and performance data from 2023 to present — shortening the path from raw campaign data to the analysis and strategy work that depends on it.

n8n · Meta Ads Manager · Google Sheets · HTML/JavaScript

Live Demo →

WHY IT MATTERED Without a single source of historical data, every monthly report meant re-opening Meta Ads Manager repeatedly and manually reconciling numbers — repetitive work that also made it harder to spot longer-term patterns across campaigns.

HOW IT WORKS Today's spend, messages, click-through rate, cost-per-click, cost-per-1000-impressions, and an account-health score are reported on the Overview page alongside budget pacing. A separate Historical Intelligence view rolls up reach, impressions, and messages by week and by month, with CSV export for reporting.

HUMAN ROLE The dashboard reports and organizes the data — campaign strategy and budget decisions stay with me.

RESULT Metric not currently tracked — reporting-time reduction has not yet been formally measured.

HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP CUSTOMER SERVICE AUTOMATION

Mitsubishi AI Messenger Assistant

BUSINESS PROBLEM There are times I can't personally answer incoming Messenger inquiries right away, and a slow first response can lose a customer's interest.

SYSTEM I BUILT An automation that receives inquiries, retrieves the information needed to respond, and uses AI to handle what it reasonably can — escalating to me through Telegram when a conversation needs human judgment.

n8n · Facebook Messenger · Telegram · AI API

See Full Workflow ↓

WHY IT MATTERED Customers reach out outside of the hours I can personally monitor Messenger. Without a first response system, inquiries sat unanswered until I was available again.

HOW IT WORKS Incoming messages are checked for duplicates and routed by type. New vs. existing customers are identified, the AI assistant generates a reply or sends the welcome carousel, lead details are extracted and logged — and at any point, I can take over the conversation directly.

HUMAN ROLE This is not a full chatbot replacement — the design principle is that AI handles what it reasonably can, and I take over when a conversation needs judgment or capability the AI doesn't have. I can switch AI → Human and Human → AI at any point.

RESULT Metric not currently tracked — response-time improvement has not yet been formally measured.

BUSINESS WORKFLOW SYSTEM

Citimotors CRM System

BUSINESS PROBLEM The sales team needed one place to track leads, follow-ups, and pipeline stage — built from years of working inside CRM and after-sales systems, not from wanting a portfolio project.

SYSTEM I BUILT A CRM covering the full sales workflow: customer inquiry, follow-up, pipeline stage, application, quotation, vehicle details and bulletins, and monitoring — connected to a live data source.

HTML · JavaScript · Google Sheets · Google Apps Script · GitHub

Live Demo →

WHY IT MATTERED Without a shared pipeline view, leads and their current stage lived in scattered notes and memory — making it easy to lose visibility on where a customer actually stood in the process.

HOW IT WORKS The Sales Pipeline Overview tracks leads through Lead, Qualified, Application, Waiting for Requirements, Waiting for Bank Approval, Approved, Released, and Lost, with week-over-week deltas per stage. A Source Breakdown panel separates AI-sourced leads (captured through the Messenger automation) from manually entered sales.

HUMAN ROLE The system organizes and surfaces the data; qualifying leads, negotiating, and closing sales remain entirely human work.

RESULT Metric not currently tracked — pipeline-visibility improvement has not yet been formally measured.

AI-ASSISTED DECISION SUPPORT

Meta AI Analyst

BUSINESS PROBLEM Campaign data by itself doesn't say what to do next — reading raw numbers and deciding on action from intuition alone isn't reliable.

SYSTEM I BUILT An automation that analyzes available Meta advertising data, surfaces patterns and performance issues, and generates decision-support ideas for what might be worth doing next.

n8n · Google Gemini · Meta Ads data · Google Sheets

RECURRING CONTENT OPERATIONS AUTOMATION

Mitsubishi Facebook Automation

BUSINESS PROBLEM Daily posting is expected Monday through Sunday, but I can't guarantee a prepared image will always be ready in the posting folder on any given day.

SYSTEM I BUILT A recurring content workflow: it posts prepared content when available, and falls back to alternate content logic when it isn't — removing the need to manually run the posting process every day.

n8n · Facebook · Telegram · Google Sheets · Gemini AI

WHY IT MATTERED Missing even one day's post breaks posting consistency, and manually preparing and posting content daily is a recurring task easy to forget or delay.

HOW IT WORKS On a schedule, the workflow sources new vehicle images, generates AI captions and post images, routes them through a Telegram approval step, then posts to Facebook and logs the result. If no ready image is available, fallback content logic takes over instead.

HUMAN ROLE I approve posts through Telegram before anything goes live — the system prepares and schedules; I retain final publishing approval.

RESULT Metric not currently tracked — the goal is reliability and continuity of the daily publishing process, not yet formally measured.
