WORKFLOW AUTOMATION

How the Messenger Automation Works

Built in n8n, based only on what the actual workflow shows — a customer-communication automation for Facebook Messenger inquiries.

01 · CUSTOMER MESSAGE IN Incoming Facebook Messenger message, checked for duplicates
→
02 · ROUTING Routed by message type or button pressed (models, requirements, application, contact)
→
03 · CUSTOMER LOOKUP New vs. existing customer identified, chat record created or restored
→
04 · AI REPLY Mitsubishi AI Assistant generates the reply, or sends the welcome carousel for new customers
→
05 · LEAD & LOG Lead details extracted and the conversation saved to the conversation log
→
06 · HUMAN TAKEOVER Admin takeover available at any point in the conversation
